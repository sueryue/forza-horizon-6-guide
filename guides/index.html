Your first 10 hours

Roadmap

Official gameplay still

Tap to play the related official Forza video

A relaxed path from the Qualifiers to a garage you actually like.

  1. 1

    Run the Qualifiers, then the Invitational

    Finishing the Horizon Invitational grants your first (Yellow) Wristband and unlocks the festival, multiplayer and free cars.

  2. 2

    Pick a starter that fits you

    Silvia K'S for drift, Celica GT-Four for balance, GMC Jimmy for off-road. All three end up in your garage anyway.

  3. 3

    Don't spend credits at the Autoshow

    Wheelspins, story events and the Festival Playlist hand out cars fast. Tune your starter instead.

  4. 4

    Photo the landmarks

    Press Up for photo mode. Landmark shots feed your Collection Journal and earn Discover Japan stamps.

  5. 5

    Three-star a few PR stunts

    Speed Zones, Danger Signs and Drift Zones pay Super Wheelspins and Skill Points for Car Mastery.

  6. 6

    Raise difficulty for credits

    Higher difficulty with assists off can pay up to +125% credits per race — once you can still win.

Unlock all 15 Barn Finds

Collectibles

Official gameplay still

Tap to play the related official Forza video

Classic cars hidden in barns — gated by exploration, not luck.

  1. 1

    Earn Discover Japan stamps

    Explore regions, find landmarks and photograph them. Stamps are what unlock barn rumours — the yellow-badge story missions pay out the most.

  2. 2

    Watch for the rumour notification

    Each of the seven stamp tiers unlocks one to four Barn Find rumours. You'll get a purple search circle, not an exact pin.

  3. 3

    Scout it with the drone

    Fly a drone over the purple circle — the moment you spot the barn it gets marked on your map, and you can drive straight to it.

  4. 4

    Restore and enjoy

    Found barns go to your garage. There are 15 across Japan, from the 2005 Honda NSX-R GT to the 1991 Mazda 787B. Restoration takes real time, so start early.

Hunt the 9 Treasure Cars

Collectibles

Official gameplay still

Tap to play the related official Forza video

Abandoned cars found only through photo clues — no marker until discovered.

  1. 1

    Get the photo clue

    Treasure clues show a snapshot of the location. Study the background — mountains, signs, road shape — to narrow it down.

  2. 2

    Match it on the map

    There is no map marker. Use landmarks and the horizon to triangulate the spot.

  3. 3

    Drive over the dig site

    Once you're close, the dig prompt appears. There are 9 Treasure Cars, one per mainland region plus Tokyo City — no stamp requirement, no restoration wait.

Drift tuning basics

Tuning

Official gameplay still

Tap to play the related official Forza video

Make almost any car slide predictably.

  1. 1

    Tires: lower the pressure

    Drop rear tire pressure a few PSI for a looser, more forgiving slide.

  2. 2

    Differential: bias to the rear

    Increase rear deceleration (and acceleration) so the back steps out on throttle lift and braking.

  3. 3

    Alignment: add caster

    More positive caster sharpens turn-in and self-centring, which keeps drifts controllable.

  4. 4

    Power & weight

    RWD with healthy power drifts best; a little ballast at the rear helps rotation. Then practice on the Touge.

Credits fund upgrades and the cars you actually want.

Features & Systems

New Gameplay Features

via Gamersky

What is new in Horizon Japan: seasons, Horizon Rush, Time Attack, Car Meets, CoLab, audio, and customization.

  • Japan setting with real landmarks (Shibuya Crossing, Ginkgo Avenue, Tokyo Tower), C1-loop-inspired roads, and passes like Haruna and Bandai-East.
  • Four distinct seasons with visual and audio changes; fit snow tires year-round to drive alpine snow.
  • New activities: Horizon Rush obstacle courses, Time Attack (gates, fastest lap), Car Meets, and a 12-car Drag Strip.
  • Build your life in Japan: a customizable garage showroom, the Estate you build in the open world, and CoLab (up to 12 players building together in EventLab).
  • Upgrades: Forza Aero, window-livery painting, separate front/rear wheels, visible tire wear, 540-degree wheel rotation, vehicle proximity radar, and Triton Acoustics spatial audio.

Three Progression Systems

via Gamersky

The three core progression tracks: Wristbands, Stamps, and Horizon Play.

  • Wristbands: 7 total; your pass to stricter-class festival events. The gold wristband unlocks Legend Island and the series' longest Goliath race.
  • Stamps: 7 earned from the 'Explore Japan' storyline, inspired by Japanese stamp-collecting culture; fill the book to unlock garage treasure rumours, properties, and the grandparents' Estate.
  • Horizon Play: online modes (The Eliminator battle royale, Hide & Seek, Touge Showdown best-of-3, Spec Racing with stock cars) plus Free Race and Horizon Drift; independent level to 100 with badges, and series-resetting leaderboards.

The Eliminator (King of the Hill) Tips

via Gamersky

Core tips for the Eliminator battle-royale mode's duel mechanics.

  • Never duel at the circle center - fight at the edge and run toward the center; your duel endpoint spawns along the diameter toward the middle.
  • If the circle covers a main road (e.g. a highway), focus duels there - run it back and forth or camp both ends.
  • In the finals with a weak car, exit opposite to the mainstream direction and hug the 3-second exit limit.
  • When chasing, don't overcommit near the edge - veterans bait you out then steal the start. If you're being chased, crash into a restroom/small house to stop them and steal a big start.

Chinese Text + English Audio

via Gamersky

Keep the Chinese UI but switch the voice-over to English (or Japanese).

  • Set game language to Simplified Chinese, then in the install's /media/Stripped/StringTables folder swap the names of CHS.zip and EN.zip (so CHS.zip now holds the English audio).
  • In C:/Users/<you>/AppData/Local/ForzaHorizon6 create a file named 'UserPreferredLang' (no extension) containing 'EN' (or 'JP' for Japanese dub).
  • Game updates can revert the files - just repeat the steps.
