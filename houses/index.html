Houses & Property

Two different sets of "8" in Horizon Japan — and mixing them up is the single most common error in FH6 property guides. Wikipedia confirms one headline property, The Estate, plus 8 Garages that double as fast-travel and car-display points [WIKI]. Separately, community guides enumerate 8 buyable player houses, each with a permanent perk that stays active wherever you spawn; Wikipedia does not enumerate those, so they are labelled community-sourced [RPS, TECH, FH6GUIDE]. The Estate (Yashiki House) is the headline — a fully buildable plot you shape with an upgraded EventLab toolset. Houses unlock through the seven Discover Japan stamp tiers (yellow Visitor → green Sightseer → blue Traveller → pink Pathfinder → orange Navigator → purple Adventurer → gold Master Explorer).

Garages — the other "8"

Horizon Japan has 8 Garages — a different set of 8 from the buyable houses above. These double as free fast-travel points and shareable car-display spaces with customisable, community-downloadable interiors [WIKI]. The Estate is the only property Wikipedia confirms; the 8 Garages are the other Wikipedia-confirmed piece of the property picture.

Don't confuse these with houses — they're fast-travel/car-display points, not owned properties.

Living in Japan

  • Unlock Mei's House first — it's free and opens car trading at the Autoshow.
  • The Estate costs just 10,000 Cr and is your permanent, buildable base; claim it early.
  • House perks stay active wherever you spawn, so owning all 8 stacks every bonus.
  • Most houses need a Discover Japan Stamp tier (yellow Visitor → gold Master Explorer) before they're buyable, and the same seven tiers gate the 15 Barn Finds.
  • Hakusan Mountain Lodge gives the best ROI: an extra garage slot plus +10% Horizon Life credits.
  • Tokyo House's daily Wheelspin is the strongest long-term credit grind (free with VIP).
  • Garages are separate fast-travel points — unlock all 8 to cross the big map in seconds.
  • At The Estate, up to 12 players co-create via Horizon Collab; browse and download community layouts.
