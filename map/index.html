Horizon Japan

Interactive Map

Forza Horizon 6 is set in a fictionalised Japan with the largest open-world map in the series [WIKI]. The areas below are named in Wikipedia; community guides report the full map spans 10 regions — we list what is encyclopaedically confirmed and clearly mark the rest.

Tap a marker to jump to that region ↓

Layers

Tokyo Confirmed

The largest urban area in any Forza Horizon game — about five times the size of previous city areas [WIKI]. Districts include Shibuya Crossing, Ginko Avenue, and Tokyo Tower, linked by elevated highways. Mix of neon downtown, suburbs, and industrial docks [WIKI].

  • Shibuya Crossing
  • Ginko Avenue
  • Tokyo Tower
  • Elevated highways
  • Neon downtown · suburbs · industrial docks
  • ≈5× the size of prior city areas [WIKI]

Legend Island Confirmed

A special area unlocked with the Gold Wristband, featuring unique challenges, exclusive events, and a second Horizon Festival [WIKI].

  • Gold Wristband unlock
  • Unique challenges
  • Exclusive events
  • Second Horizon Festival

Rural Japan Confirmed

The countryside, home to the Estate — your customisable mountainside property [WIKI]. Scenic driving and overlooks.

  • The Estate (customisable property)
  • Mountainous scenery
  • Open-world building

Mount Fuji Confirmed

Japan's iconic peak appears as a backdrop behind Tokyo and the key art — a landmark rather than a drivable district [WIKI].

  • Backdrop landmark
  • Visible from Tokyo
  • Key-art centerpiece

Hokkaido Reported

Community-reported northern region — snow-covered tundra and ice-road driving. Named by community guides; not officially confirmed by Wikipedia.

  • Community: snow & ice driving
  • Northernmost area
  • Contributes to Discover Japan stamps

PR stunts & activities

  • Speed Traps — Flash through a gate above a target speed. Three-star for Super Wheelspins and Skill Points.
  • Speed Zones — Hold a target average speed across a measured stretch of road.
  • Danger Signs — Launch off a ramp and fly as far as the jump will carry you.
  • Drift Zones — Bank the highest drift score before you cross the finish line.
  • Trailblazers — Point-to-point sprint against the clock — the shortest, smoothest line wins.

Exploring tips

  • Fast-travel to any road you've already driven — explore first, blink later.
  • Discover Japan stamps unlock Barn Find rumors and feed the Collection Journal.
  • Photo spots (PH) double as Collection Journal stamps — stop and shoot them as you pass.
  • Garages (G) are free fast-travel points; unlock all 8 early to cross the huge map in seconds.
