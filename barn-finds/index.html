Barn Finds are classic cars abandoned in barns across Japan, gated behind your Discover Japan stamps. There are 15 of them, unlocked in batches of one to four as you climb the seven Discover Japan stamp tiers from Visitor to Master Explorer. Wikipedia does not enumerate this mechanic, so the roster below is guide-sourced and cross-checked between IGN, Windows Central, PC Gamer and Red Bull.

All 15 Barn Finds

In the order the Discover Japan stamp tiers unlock them. Locations are described by landmark, the way the source guides do — we do not publish invented coordinates. We list all 15 Barn Finds; some interactive maps still show only 14 (a stale count).

How to find them

  1. 1

    Earn Discover Japan stamps — mascots smashed, food delivery jobs, street and touge races, and above all the yellow-badge story missions.

  2. 2

    Each stamp tier unlocks new Barn Find rumours (generally one to four at a time).

  3. 3

    A purple circle appears on the map — drive into it to reveal the barn's rough area.

  4. 4

    Search the circle for the barn; you can spot it fast by flying a Drone over the zone.

  5. 5

    The car is sent for restoration, which takes real time — a couple of hours up to several days. You can pay credits to skip it, but it is rarely worth it.

Honest note: We deliberately give descriptive landmark pointers, not map coordinates — the four guides we cross-checked describe these spots in words, and inventing coordinate pairs would be exactly the kind of fabrication this site avoids.
