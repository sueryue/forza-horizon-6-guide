Treasure Cars are abandoned vehicles hidden across Japan, found only via photo clues (no map marker until you are nearly on top of one). There are nine — one per region of the mainland, with the ninth in Tokyo City. Unlike Barn Finds they need no stamp progression and no restoration: just drive up and claim them, in any order. Wikipedia does not enumerate this mechanic, so the roster below is guide-sourced and cross-checked across four outlets.

How to find them

  1. 1

    Drive around Japan; Mei hands you a photo clue when you pass near a Treasure Car.

  2. 2

    The photo is the puzzle — study the background (mountains, signs, road shape) to narrow it down.

  3. 3

    Find the exact spot, claim the car, and it's in your collection for free — no restoration wait.

  4. 4

    Once discovered, the location stays marked on your map.

  5. 5

    A systematic north-to-south sweep covers all nine in a couple of hours.

Honest note: All nine are free. GamingBolt reports that claiming every Treasure Car unlocks the 'Lost and Found' badge — treat that one as single-sourced. We show GamingBolt's map-grid label (letter+number, e.g. D 234) for each car as a navigation aid — that is their overlay grid, not an in-game GPS readout, and we still publish landmark descriptions rather than invented coordinates.
